## Honest gaps

1. **Russia**: sovereignty and sanctions context — per EU / US / UK / other
   applicable sanctions regimes, deployment restrictions may apply.
   Architecturally jurisdiction-neutral CC BY 4.0 documentation does
   not constitute compliance with any sanctioning regime.
2. **Belarus**: sovereignty and sanctions context — same as Russia.
3. **Kazakhstan**: AIFC (Astana International Financial Centre) has a
   separate AI governance framework for financialcentre participants;
   per-jurisdiction file notes this distinction.
4. Enforcement of algorithmic transparency and accountability
   obligations in Russia and Belarus is inconsistent and non-public;
   per-deployment compliance determination is deployer responsibility
   and must account for applicable sanctions regimes.

## How to use this matrix

1. **Forward (architecture → regulation)**: pick an Invariant. Read across.
   The cell shows where the architectural property engages the framework.
2. **Backward (regulation → architecture)**: pick a jurisdiction column.
   Read down. Every Invariant that engages that jurisdiction's framework
   is shown.
3. **Substantive detail**: each cell points at the relevant article,
   principle, section, or standard. Full mapping in the jurisdiction file.
