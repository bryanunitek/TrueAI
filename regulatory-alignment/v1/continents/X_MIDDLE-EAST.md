## How to use this matrix

1. **Forward (architecture → regulation)**: pick an Invariant. Read across.
   The cell shows where the architectural property engages the framework.
2. **Backward (regulation → architecture)**: pick a jurisdiction column.
   Read down. Every Invariant that engages that jurisdiction's framework
   is shown.
3. **Substantive detail**: each cell points at the relevant article,
   principle, section, or standard. Full mapping in the jurisdiction file.
