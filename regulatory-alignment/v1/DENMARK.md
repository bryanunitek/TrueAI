# Denmark — National AI Framework

How the public corpus aligns with Denmark's AI regulatory framework
including the EU AI Act (directly applicable), the National Strategy for
AI, Datatilsynet oversight, and sector frameworks.

## The law

### 1. Supra-national: EU AI Act directly applicable

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 applies directly. See `EU-AI-ACT.md`.

### 2. National AI strategy

- **National Strategy for AI (Strategi for kunstig intelligens)** —
  Ministry of Industry, Business and Financial Affairs + Ministry of
  Finance, 2019; updated 2024.
- **Digital Strategy 2022-2026**.
- **Danish Centre for AI Innovation**.

### 3. Data protection layer

- **Databeskyttelsesloven (Act No. 502 of 23 May 2018)** — implements
  GDPR.
- **Datatilsynet** — supervisory authority; established Danish guidance
  on AI in employment, public-sector AI, AI in healthcare.

### 4. Cybersecurity layer

- **NIS2 transposition (Lov om net- og informationssikkerhed for
  væsentlige enheder)** — passed 2025.
- **Center for Cybersikkerhed (CFCS)** under FE.
- **Cyber Security Council**.

### 5. Sector-specific frameworks

- **Finanstilsynet (DFSA)** — financial sector AI; aligned with EBA
  guidance.
- **Lægemiddelstyrelsen (DKMA)** — AI medical devices.
- **Sundhedsdatastyrelsen** — health data AI.

### 6. Equality framework

- **Forskelsbehandlingsloven (Act on the Prohibition of Discrimination
  on the Labour Market)**.
- **Institut for Menneskerettigheder (Danish Institute for Human
  Rights)** — algorithmic discrimination monitoring.

## Provisions in scope and how the corpus fulfils each

EU AI Act provisions: see `EU-AI-ACT.md`.

Danish-specific additions: Datatilsynet's published AI guidance on
employment, public sector, healthcare; Finanstilsynet AI/ML
expectations; algorithmic discrimination guidance from Institut for
Menneskerettigheder.

**How the corpus fulfils these**: Same architectural mapping as EU AI
Act baseline plus the Singular Pairing Principle for accountability in
public-sector AI deployments.

## Honest gaps

1. Danish competent authority designation for EU AI Act ongoing.
2. Public-sector AI projects (e.g. Gladsaxe-modellen historical) have
   produced significant policy debate; precedent for tight oversight.
3. **Vertical CORE.Law December 2026 prototype, December 2027 first
   conformant production demonstration** — see `README.md` cross-cutting
   honest gaps.
