# Belarus — National AI Framework

How the public corpus aligns with Belarus's AI regulatory framework
including the National AI roadmap, Personal Data Protection Law 99-Z, and sector frameworks. Sovereignty and sanctions context: international sanctions affect deployment and cross-border data flow. Per-deployment Belarusian-residency determination is deployer responsibility.

## The law

### 1. National AI strategy

- **Decree of the President of the Republic of Belarus on AI development (2020); national digital transformation framework**.

### 2. Data protection layer

- **Law No. 99-Z of 7 May 2021 on Personal Data Protection (effective 15 November 2021)**.
- **National Personal Data Protection Centre** — supervisory authority.

### 3. Cybersecurity layer

- **Concept of Information Security; Operational Analytical Centre (OAC)**.
- **CERT.BY**.

### 4. Sector-specific frameworks

- **National Bank of the Republic of Belarus (NBRB)** — financial sector AI.
- **Centre for Examinations and Tests in Health Service** — AI medical devices.

### 5. Equality framework

- **Law on Prevention of Discrimination**.

## Provisions in scope and how the corpus fulfils each

Principles map onto Nine Invariants and 12 Levels.

### Cybersecurity

### Equality

architectural level.

## Honest gaps

1. Belarus has no AI-specific horizontal law yet (strategy/policy
   only) unless explicitly noted otherwise above.
2. Enforcement precedent for AI-specific failures is developing.
3. Sectoral overlay from central bank, health regulator, and ICT
   regulator applies in addition to the cross-cutting data protection
   statute.
4. **Vertical CORE.Law December 2026 prototype, December 2027 first
   conformant production demonstration** — see `README.md` cross-cutting
   honest gaps.
