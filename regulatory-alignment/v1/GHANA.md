# Ghana — National AI Framework

How the public corpus aligns with Ghana's AI regulatory framework
including the National AI Strategy, the Data Protection Act, and sector
frameworks.

## The law

### 1. National AI strategy

- **Ghana National Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2023-2033** —
  Ministry of Communications and Digitalisation; sets national AI
  priorities.
- **Ghana-AI** initiative coordinated by NITA.
- **National AI Ethics Committee** under development.

### 2. Data protection layer

- **Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843)** — Ghana's comprehensive data
  protection statute.
- **Data Protection Commission (DPC)** — supervisory authority.

### 3. Cybersecurity layer

- **Cybersecurity Act 2020 (Act 1038)**.
- **Cyber Security Authority (CSA)** — established under Act 1038.
- **National Cybersecurity Centre**.

### 4. Sector-specific frameworks

- **Bank of Ghana (BoG)** — financial sector AI; Sandbox Regulatory
  Framework.
- **Securities and Exchange Commission Ghana (SEC)**.
- **Food and Drugs Authority (FDA Ghana)** — AI medical devices.
- **National Communications Authority (NCA)** — AI in
  telecommunications.

### 5. Equality framework

- **Constitution 1992 Article 17** — non-discrimination.
- **Labour Act 2003 (Act 651)** — applies to AI in employment.
- **Persons with Disability Act 2006**.

## Provisions in scope and how the corpus fulfils each

### Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843)

Act 843 provides comprehensive data protection including: principles;
data subject rights; cross-border transfer; DPC oversight.

**How the corpus fulfils this**: Maps onto the same architectural
properties that satisfy GDPR.

### Ghana National AI Strategy 2023-2033

Strategy sets priorities including: AI ecosystem; AI for development;
governance and ethics; talent; data infrastructure.

**How the corpus fulfils this**: The architecture is documentation
compatible with the strategy's governance-and-ethics priority.

### BoG — financial sector AI

BoG guidance on fintech and AI in banking.

**How the corpus fulfils this**: Same architectural mapping that
satisfies financial sector frameworks elsewhere.

### FDA Ghana — AI medical devices

FDA Ghana regulates AI as medical device.

**How the corpus fulfils this**: Same architectural mapping as German
MPDG, French HAS sections.

### Cybersecurity Act 2020 + CSA

CSA oversees CII and cybersecurity baselines.

**How the corpus fulfils this**: UniCORE.GVB substrate provides
technical baselines.

### Constitution + Labour Act

Apply to AI affecting protected characteristics.

**How the corpus fulfils this**: Invariants 4 and 6 structurally
prohibit discriminatory AI behaviour at architectural level.

## Honest gaps

1. **No AI-specific horizontal law**: Strategy is policy.

2. **Data Protection Act 2012 modernisation pending**: amendment bills
   under consideration to align with international developments.

3. **Sectoral overlay**: BoG, SEC, FDA Ghana, NCA apply sectoral
   overlays.

4. **Vertical CORE.Law December 2026 prototype, December 2027 first
   conformant production demonstration** — see `README.md` cross-cutting
   honest gaps.
