# Bosnia and Herzegovina — National AI Framework

How the public corpus aligns with Bosnia and Herzegovina's AI regulatory framework
including EU candidate alignment, draft Strategy on AI Development in BiH, Law on Personal Data Protection, and sector frameworks. Federal structure (Federation of BiH + Republika Srpska + Brčko District) creates multi-tier coordination.

## The law

### 1. National AI strategy

- **Draft Strategy on AI Development in BiH (in development)**.

### 2. Data protection layer

- **Law on Personal Data Protection of Bosnia and Herzegovina (Official Gazette 49/06, 76/11, 89/11)**.
- **Personal Data Protection Agency of BiH** — supervisory authority.

### 3. Cybersecurity layer

- **Law on Information Security**.
- **BiH CERT**.

### 4. Sector-specific frameworks

- **Centralna banka Bosne i Hercegovine (CBBH)** — financial sector AI.
- **Agency for Medicinal Products and Medical Devices (ALMBIH)** — AI medical devices.

### 5. Equality framework

- **Law on Prohibition of Discrimination**.

## Honest gaps

1. Bosnia and Herzegovina has no AI-specific horizontal law yet (strategy/policy
   only) unless explicitly noted otherwise above.
2. Enforcement precedent for AI-specific failures is developing.
3. Sectoral overlay from central bank, health regulator, and ICT
   regulator applies in addition to the cross-cutting data protection
   statute.
4. **Vertical CORE.Law December 2026 prototype, December 2027 first
   conformant production demonstration** — see `README.md` cross-cutting
   honest gaps.
