# Austria — National AI Framework

How the public corpus aligns with Austria's AI regulatory framework
including the EU AI Act (directly applicable), the AIM AT 2030 Strategy,
DSB oversight, and sector frameworks.

## The law

### 1. Supra-national: EU AI Act directly applicable

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 applies directly. See `EU-AI-ACT.md`.

### 2. National AI strategy

- **AIM AT 2030 — Austrian Artificial Intelligence Strategy** —
  BMK/BMDW/BMBWF, 2021.
- **AI Mission Austria 2030 — KI-Strategie**.
- **Austrian Council on Robotics and Artificial Intelligence (ACRAI)**.

### 3. Data protection layer

- **Datenschutzgesetz (DSG)** — implements GDPR.
- **Datenschutzbehörde (DSB)** — supervisory authority; has issued AI
  and profiling guidance.

### 4. Cybersecurity layer

- **Netz- und Informationssystemsicherheitsgesetz (NISG)** transposing
  NIS2 (2024).
- **GovCERT Austria** and **CERT.at**.

### 5. Sector-specific frameworks

- **Finanzmarktaufsicht (FMA)** — financial sector AI; aligned with
  EBA/EIOPA/ESMA.
- **Oesterreichische Nationalbank (OeNB)** — central bank.
- **BASG (AGES)** — AI medical devices.

### 6. Equality framework

- **Gleichbehandlungsgesetz** + **Gleichbehandlungsanwaltschaft
  (Ombud for Equal Treatment)**.

## Provisions in scope and how the corpus fulfils each

EU AI Act provisions: see `EU-AI-ACT.md`.

Austrian-specific additions: DSB GDPR Article 22 and profiling
enforcement; FMA AI/ML expectations; ACRAI advisory on responsible AI.

**How the corpus fulfils these**: Same architectural mapping as EU AI
Act baseline + German BDSG/BSI mapping (DACH overlap).

## Honest gaps

1. Austrian competent authority designation for EU AI Act ongoing
   (BMK candidate).
2. Bundesländer-level digital initiatives create coordination overhead.
3. **Vertical CORE.Law December 2026 prototype, December 2027 first
   conformant production demonstration** — see `README.md` cross-cutting
   honest gaps.
