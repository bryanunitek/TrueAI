# Rwanda — National AI Framework

How the public corpus aligns with Rwanda's AI regulatory framework
including the National AI Policy 2023, DP Law 058/2021, and sector frameworks. Rwanda is a Smart Africa Alliance leader.

## The law

### 1. National AI strategy

- **Rwanda National AI Policy 2023 (Ministry of ICT and Innovation)**.

### 2. Data protection layer

- **Law 058/2021 on the Protection of Personal Data and Privacy**.
- **National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA)** — supervisory authority.

### 3. Cybersecurity layer

- **Cyber Security Strategy**.
- **Rw-CSIRT**.

### 4. Sector-specific frameworks

- **National Bank of Rwanda (BNR)** — financial sector AI.
- **Rwanda FDA** — AI medical devices.

### 5. Equality framework

- **Constitution Article 15 + Labour Law**.

## Provisions in scope and how the corpus fulfils each

Data Protection maps onto the same architectural properties that satisfy
GDPR. Singular Pairing Principle structures accountability; TrueAI
Invariant 9 routes legally-significant decisions through the named
human.

National AI Strategy principles map onto Nine Invariants and 12 Levels.

Financial sector AI maps onto SS1/23 UK, MaRisk Germany, FREE-AI India
architectural mapping.

AI medical devices map onto German MPDG, French HAS architectural
mapping.

Cybersecurity: UniCORE.GVB substrate provides technical baselines.

Equality: Invariants 4 and 6 structurally prohibit discriminatory AI
behaviour at architectural level.

## Honest gaps

1. Rwanda has no AI-specific horizontal law yet (strategy/policy
   only) unless explicitly noted otherwise above.
2. Enforcement precedent for AI-specific failures is developing.
3. Sectoral overlay applies in addition to cross-cutting statute.
4. **Vertical CORE.Law December 2026 prototype, December 2027 first
   conformant production demonstration** — see `README.md` cross-cutting
   honest gaps.
