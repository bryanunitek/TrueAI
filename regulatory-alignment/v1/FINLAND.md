# Finland — National AI Framework

How the public corpus aligns with Finland's AI regulatory framework
including the EU AI Act (directly applicable), the AuroraAI programme,
Tietosuojavaltuutettu oversight, and sector frameworks.

## The law

### 1. Supra-national: EU AI Act directly applicable

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 applies directly. See `EU-AI-ACT.md`.

### 2. National AI strategy

- **Finland's Age of Artificial Intelligence (Suomen Tekoälyaika)** —
  Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment, 2017; foundational
  Finnish AI strategy (one of Europe's earliest).
- **AuroraAI Programme** — national AI applications programme
  (2018-2022, succeeded by follow-on).
- **Government Resolution on Promoting AI 2022-2027**.

### 3. Data protection layer

- **Tietosuojalaki (Data Protection Act 1050/2018)** — implements GDPR.
- **Tietosuojavaltuutettu (Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman)** —
  supervisory authority.

### 4. Cybersecurity layer

- **NIS2-transponointi** — Finnish NIS2 transposition 2024.
- **Kyberturvallisuuskeskus** (NCSC-FI) under Traficom.

### 5. Sector-specific frameworks

- **Finanssivalvonta (FIN-FSA)** — financial sector AI; aligned with
  EBA/EIOPA/ESMA guidance.
- **Fimea** — AI medical devices.
- **Valvira** — healthcare quality.

### 6. Equality framework

- **Yhdenvertaisuuslaki (Non-Discrimination Act 1325/2014)**.
- **Yhdenvertaisuusvaltuutettu (Non-Discrimination Ombudsman)** —
  algorithmic discrimination guidance.

## Provisions in scope and how the corpus fulfils each

EU AI Act provisions: see `EU-AI-ACT.md`.

Finnish-specific additions: Tietosuojavaltuutettu AI/profiling
guidance; FIN-FSA AI/ML expectations; AuroraAI public-sector AI
governance model.

**How the corpus fulfils these**: Same architectural mapping as EU AI
Act + Sweden (sibling Nordic).

## Honest gaps

1. Finnish competent authority designation for EU AI Act ongoing.
2. AuroraAI programme governance model is public-sector specific and
   has limited applicability to commercial deployments.
3. **Vertical CORE.Law December 2026 prototype, December 2027 first
   conformant production demonstration** — see `README.md` cross-cutting
   honest gaps.
