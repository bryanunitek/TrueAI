# Monitoring List — Jurisdictions without dedicated regulatory-alignment files yet

This list covers the UN member states (plus a small number of non-UN
jurisdictions of regulatory relevance) that **do not yet have a
dedicated jurisdiction file** in this `regulatory-alignment/v1/` shelf.

These jurisdictions are being **monitored** for one of three triggers
that would cause a dedicated file to be authored:

1. **Enactment or substantial progression of an AI-specific law** (e.g.
   horizontal AI act, AI-in-public-services act, AI ethics binding code).
2. **Enactment of a comprehensive Personal Data Protection Law** with
   AI-relevant provisions (automated decision-making, profiling, large-
   scale processing).
3. **Adoption of a national AI Strategy** with implementation milestones
   that affect the regulatory environment for deployers.

When any of those triggers fires, the jurisdiction is promoted from this
list to a dedicated `<JURISDICTION>.md` file in this shelf and added to
the matrix coverage tables.

## Discussions invitation — corrections welcome

This list is best-effort and may contain inaccuracies, omissions, or
out-of-date entries. If you spot any, please open a thread in the public
Discussions of the relevant sibling repo (UniVERSE, TrueAI, UniCORE-AI,
UniCORE, UniCORE.GVB, UniSaaS.UniCORE, UniSaaS.UniCORE.GVB).

Specifically welcome:

- **Country-promotion request**: a country on this monitoring list now
  has a binding AI law, comprehensive PDPL, or substantive AI strategy
  worth dedicated-file treatment.
- **Inaccuracy report**: an existing dedicated file misstates a law,
  date, authority, or scope.
- **Missing-country report**: a country not in any list and not in any
  dedicated file should be added.
- **Source-improvement**: a more authoritative or up-to-date source for
  a cited regulation.

We will respond and the corpus will be updated. The shelf is licensed
CC BY 4.0; suggested edits are equally welcome via the standard public
contribution path.

## Aggregate files

Three aggregate jurisdiction files in this shelf cover regions where
multiple states share a common framework and are best treated together
rather than per-state at this stage:

- `GCC-QATAR-KUWAIT-BAHRAIN-OMAN.md` — Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman
  (the four GCC members not having dedicated files; Saudi Arabia and
  UAE have their own dedicated files).

(Two further aggregate files may be authored as the monitoring list
matures: a Pacific Islands aggregate and a Central Asia aggregate.)

## Acknowledgements

Some monitoring-list jurisdictions have ECOWAS / SADC / OECS / Pacific
Islands Forum / CIS / EEU / Arab League / African Union / ECCAS
regional digital agendas that touch AI; per-state files will note the
applicable regional framework when authored.

If your jurisdiction is on this monitoring list and you maintain (or
work in) a national AI strategy, AI-relevant data protection law, or
emerging AI bill — we would be especially grateful for a Discussions
thread pointing us at the authoritative public source.
