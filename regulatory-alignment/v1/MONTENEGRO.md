# Montenegro — National AI Framework

How the public corpus aligns with Montenegro's AI regulatory framework
including EU candidate alignment, Strategy for AI 2024-2027, Law on Personal Data Protection, and sector frameworks.

## The law

### 1. National AI strategy

- **Strategy for AI Development 2024-2027**.

### 2. Data protection layer

- **Law on Personal Data Protection (Official Gazette of Montenegro 79/08, 70/09, 44/12, 22/17)**.
- **Agency for Personal Data Protection and Free Access to Information (AZLP)** — supervisory authority.

### 3. Cybersecurity layer

- **Law on Information Security**.
- **CIRT Montenegro**.

### 4. Sector-specific frameworks

- **Centralna banka Crne Gore (CBCG)** — financial sector AI.
- **CALIMS** — AI medical devices.

### 5. Equality framework

- **Law on Prohibition of Discrimination**.

## Honest gaps

1. Montenegro has no AI-specific horizontal law yet (strategy/policy
   only) unless explicitly noted otherwise above.
2. Enforcement precedent for AI-specific failures is developing.
3. Sectoral overlay from central bank, health regulator, and ICT
   regulator applies in addition to the cross-cutting data protection
   statute.
4. **Vertical CORE.Law December 2026 prototype, December 2027 first
   conformant production demonstration** — see `README.md` cross-cutting
   honest gaps.
